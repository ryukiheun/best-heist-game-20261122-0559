HOW TO PLAY

You are the thief. Take all 3 loot pieces,
then reach the VAULT door EXIT before the alarm locks it down.

WASD / Arrow keys — move
Shift — sneak (slower, harder to spot, quieter)
Click — throw a coin; guards investigate the CLINK
Q / Right click — jam the nearest camera for 6 s (3 per floor)
Velvet curtains — hide inside, guards and cameras cannot see you
Marble pillars — block sight and movement
Glass cases — block movement, but guards see over them

Gold cone: patrolling.  Orange '?': suspicious.
Red '!': chasing — run and break line of sight!
Red camera cones feed the ALARM meter. At 100% the vault locks down for 10 s and the exit shuts.
Guards also hear footsteps — the faint ring around you is how far they hear (70 px walking, 26 px sneaking). The SPOTTED bar shows the worst guard's suspicion before the '?' appears.
The first 3 s of a floor are safe — no guard or camera sees you.
If a guard touches you, you are CAUGHT.
